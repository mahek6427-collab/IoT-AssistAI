Frequently Asked Questions

Common IoT Questions

Why won't my IoT device connect to 5 GHz Wi-Fi? ▼
Most IoT devices only support the 2.4 GHz band due to lower cost chipsets and longer range requirements. Check your router settings and ensure you have a separate 2.4 GHz SSID available. Dual-band routers sometimes combine both bands — try separating them in your router admin panel.
How do I factory reset my IoT device safely? ▼
Before factory resetting: (1) Back up device configuration if the app supports it, (2) Note your current firmware version, (3) Remove the device from your smart home platform. Then hold the reset button for 10–15 seconds until LEDs indicate reset mode. Re-pair and re-configure after reset.
What should I do if a firmware update bricked my device? ▼
Try recovery mode first: hold Reset + Power simultaneously for 15 seconds. This forces the bootloader. Then connect via USB and use the manufacturer's recovery tool to reflash the firmware. If recovery mode is unavailable, contact manufacturer support with your device serial number.
How do Zigbee, Z-Wave, and Matter differ? ▼
Zigbee: Mesh network, 2.4 GHz, low power, requires a hub. Good for large deployments. Z-Wave: 900 MHz (less interference), mesh, requires hub, 232 device limit. Matter: New standard (2022) built on IP, works over Thread/Wi-Fi, cross-platform compatible, no hub needed. Matter is the future of smart home interoperability.
How often should I update IoT device firmware? ▼
Enable automatic updates if available for security patches. For manual updates, check monthly or when you experience issues. Always read the release notes before updating production devices — some updates may reset configurations or change device behavior.
